Thus Tëhkswen evaded the Stadrils ( Fixing Forms / Solids ) restrained by rocks stoning him. Minzhijë, for her part, sought her lost senses of the mysterious Esthrils ( Manifesting Forms / Phenomena ) hidden in colored efflorescences.
  • Wanting thus to rid himself of his hold, Sôm alone punished them by flaying them violently from Hael, exiling them to Féos. Tëhkswen's flesh entangled upon Hael tore from his bones when trying to escape, heaping into his mountains to scar, and whose skeleton shaped the four major terrestrial Rildées, the KWERYAS GJUAJ ( "Giants" of Weapons ) who also armed themselves with them to close and guard all that is precious as Beingsts charge themselves today.
  • Each animal Beingsts ( called "animals" ) was drawn upon the beaches through one of Saksag's unique eyes. With his sharpened hairs, he cut for each their unique and first fur and burrow, offering protection and identity in the vast world.
  • Wild Beingsts ( also called "creatures" or "monstrous" ), were discovered thanks to Tulsug's canine piercings, who, seeking buried treasures, felt the ground. Those with the most resistant skulls survived and were delicately inhaled through one of his nostrils. His benevolent sneeze, mixed with his vivifying snot, gave them form and intelligent appearance. Without inner murmurs to guide them, they embraced the purity of instinct, behaving with purified freedom at every instant.
  • Primitive Beingsts were born when Hegseg made his nails resonate, incising rocky walls. From his vibrating melodies, certain crystals of his long ears ossi- fied before tumbling down, giving life to these beings. Sometimes, the latter hear - in their greatest troubles - still the echoes of these original melodies that no one else hears, guiding them be- yond their sovereign instinct, thus creating the first disagreements and the first nations, between discordance and understanding.
  • Asmund crushed with his stone the clays of valleys, shaping them with expert fingers into those we today call inspired Beingsts ( such as the Slaadéens or the Tchalk- chaïs ). He transmitted a part of his knowledge to each by shaking his reproving and wisdom-filled fingers upon their heads, crushing into them his precious counsel. Thus, they could act by them- selves listening to their own voice still resonating from the first shakes, in order to build and raise their societies.

Saksag with his Hair-Cleaver cutting in Ardarie all beaches and all skins. He draws and erases with his 72 eyes the walls of each being's countries who partition and protect themselves there.

Tulsug with his Canine-Pick piercing in Aunarie all pits and all carapaces, He studies and senses with his 24 trailing nostrils upon the ground the flaws of each one's ramparts, which all invade in order to take the enclosure.

Hegseg with his Nail-Cutter incising in Frimarie all cliffs and all bones. He classifies, binds and separates with his mercantile hearing of his 8 long flattened ears the fortifications of one and the other, forming alliances and rivalries.

And finally Asmund the greatest of the four, with his Brow-Stone crushing in Naîstredor all valleys and all skulls. He flattens with the touch of 4 massive thumbs and index fingers the ruins of ancient walls, to edify from their stones his solid and purified counsel.

It is told that Asmund, Hegseg, Tulsug and Saksag still live (but where?), and that they still shape new Beingsts here and there. Sometimes, one amuses himself with the games of the others, such as inspiring animal Beingsts with counsel, or forever awakening the wild nature of certain primitive Beingsts, always enriching the world with new perspectives and flourishing cultures.

  • Minzhijë, through her whirling, scattered all her fertile seeds and dusts she found in flowered beds, thus giving all tastes, perfumes, melodies, colors, textiles and other phenomena with which Hael now loves so much to adorn herself until birthing the eight major underground Rildées, the HERWÂS THARS ( "Dwarves" of Tools ) who after feeling them, revolted from these trials Hael offered, just as the Aïares descending from them would have done ( or even descend directly ).
  • The Aïars speak to us of the Herwâs Thars as gigantic strangers ( though named as dwarves, and also their stories seem to link them to the Aïares them- selves ), nevertheless they say that each among them lived their whole life or almost in the different holes of the world and that it is there their Artefacts would still reside ( see also their corpse, some even thinking they are still alive ). Thus here are their places ( without knowing more ): they would have inhabited an ordinary cave, a sulfurous fumarole, a mine or tunnel ( dug by whom or what? ), a chasm, an under - sea cavern, a crypt ( for whom? ) a cleft, and finally an oubliette ( how long would he have remained and for what fault? ).
  • Up there, after each 15th Dusk ( Solar Term of 15 Days / Heliorée ), the immense Tëhkswen the third, mends from the mountains before lightning shatters him anew.
  • And after each 30th Dawn ( 2 Heliorées / 6 Pentaines ), Minzhijë the sixth, trades one of Hael's hidden remedies, which healed one of her lost senses, but alas, plunged another into another sickness.

Shdrazd with his Splitter breaks rocky teeth in the depths of Hael's rocky mouth to build their roofs which he perfects in Soliarque, shoring their towers so high.

Hmarlib with his Crane draws the bright entrails from Hael's inflamed guts to equip himself with loot before navigating in Ardostel, trimming their sails so swollen with their needs.

Warwuag with his Wheel shapes clayey bloods at the edges of Hael's moist skin to form from clay their visions, which they lift and move in Lunésie by their unbridled chariots.

Zhahzugodaïz with his Ramp climbs to the highest of Hael's impregnable shoulders' incessant body to see the extent of their possibilities, hoisting in Névarie their necessary burdens.

Pfaftukotaïs with his Winch tears hard hairs from Hael's leafy pores to sculpt their ships which they anchor or let float in Lunéarque according to their travel plans.

Pompoak with his Scale weighs favored eggs hanging from Hael's most fragile parts to estimate the value of their garments which they weave in Frimastel of familiar motifs and colored threads.

Nrutnap with his Gable shows the framed skeleton under Hael's flayed body to reveal its treasures, and to point their own arrival in Solésie before they are even there.

And finally, Shtrult with his Auger bores loved organs protected by Hael's bone armor to bring the waters with which they irrigate in Mûrssarie their works animated by their observations.
