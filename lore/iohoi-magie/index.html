iôHôi, sôIôs, môSôm, hôMôh and Magic

Page 121

Far from the quarrels and feasting of her children and at each of her undulations, Ô is thus eter- nally cradled and shaken between pain and relief of her magnanimous - torturers, orchestrating her symphony to the HISM.

  • iôHôi the lightning - perfuming, sôIôs the undulating - honeying, môSôm the scree - blooming and hôMôh the blazing - frosting are the four king and queen - artists of Ô, who manipulate and play her singular thread stretched into the melody of the HISM inspiring them, thus slaves to her vitality and prisoners of her hold.
  • From before and after, these tetraarchs in turn and from one of their heads to the other, torture and relieve Ô. Each of them, on all sides, express their virtuosity through their dances by whirling, pulling, aligning, twisting her and inversely. They are those who thus cause and each in their ways through movement, the different reasons behind all rilic fantasies of Ô, our world.

The sons of Éo and oÀ braiding the cord of ÉoÀ

From these facts, unique Ô is entirely braided in two family strands - Éo (Electrified) and oÀ (Magnetized) - by the vibrant melody of her four master - artists:

Through their four consolations, Ô twists from her tears of ecstasy into her first strand - Éo her first son - while through their four torments, she bends from her laughs of suffering into her second strand - oÀ her first daughter.

  • Ô thus finds her joy in pain and suffering, where each shattered debris of oÀ glitters with disillusion, grief, ingratitude and all Ô's desires. But Ô also finds her misfortune in relief and pleasure, where each tear shed of Éo is crystallized with wonder, happiness, gratitude and all her interpretations.
  • Ô perceives the spark of light emanating from the darkest moments of her past, but she also hides from shadows cast by monuments exalting her future - indeed, her joy sometimes leading to her misfortune - but also inversely - her misfortune sometimes leading to her joy, and one and the other gathering in love or repelling in hate the RIL - CENDRÉES ( Atoms / Spirits ) spirits of one another.
  • Thus then Éo and oÀ, Ô's two strands, twist into ÉoÀ ( Enluminée / Energized ), the unifying and energizing cord weaving the web binding all riches and goods of Ô with one another. Though Éo and oÀ intertwine into ÉoÀ and stretch it, the latter never intertwines with other parts of itself.
  • When Ô thus braided from Éo's cheer and oÀ's melan- choly by iôHôi - sôIôs - môSôm - hôMôh her master - slaves, ÉoÀ, from emotional torsion, moves energized and shines as a luminiferous cord.
  • It is ÉoÀ which through Ô's emotions and through Ril-cendrées trembles with light, guiding through its tensions in order to enlighten us as much by the immense and ponc- tual sun, as by stars whose traits disappear in shadows or beside sparks wandering from their torches.
