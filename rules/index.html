Rules overview

The Discording Tales tabletop system is rules heavy and procedures light - built so alien cultures stay playable. Interactive tools live on the full site; these pages are the indexable map.

How the system hangs together

Characters are built from peoples, moralities, attributes, and aptitudes. The world is not a human default: peoples and lore tell you what competence and desire mean before dice do. Eight interaction paths (Battle & Debate, Infiltration & Investigation, Craft & Enigma, Travel & Feast) structure how tables touch the underworld.
