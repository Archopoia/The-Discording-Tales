Peoples of The Discording Tales

In the crust, it was said that once lived the Aryohpeans, now remembered as beings of light, and as makers of great artifacts, or even of magic itself. Digging still deeper they reached SIWÔMIZ, a cosmo-crystalline forest where the laws of matter invert. Through that forest pulses the Ril, the braid that permeates everything that exists. Before they went further in and disappeared, they prepared three departures. Now we remember them as the Aïars. Some claim the peoples of today come from them, as echoes of their furthest possibilities.

  • Aristese

    Sovereign warriors; growth and archives. Trepharchy (Sovereign Growth)

  • Greyscribes

    Nested sages; mangroves and truths. Mignusophy (Nested Wisdom)

  • Navillis

    Maritime tribes; boat-gardens, joy. Olbophely (Devoted Felicity)

  • Meridians

    Prove worth by action; austèles, Labyrinthe. Eteathly (Competitive Authenticity)

  • Great Ylves

    Great Ylves; mastery and creation. Edapoesy (Creative Competence)

  • Pale Ylves

    Pale Ylves; gain and regency. Hieragogy (Sacred Direction)

  • Lake Ylves

    Semi-sedentary; lake and forest, ingenuity. Mellophrony (Ingenious Volition)

  • Iqqars

    Inner experience; reduced dimorphism. Egonexy (Extrinsic Intimism)

  • Slaadeans

    Bold dreamers; life 2 - 115 years. Thrasoniry (Dreaming Audacity)

  • Tchalkchaïs

    Sacrificial altruists; life 10 - 27 years. Echotysy (Sacrificial Altruism)
