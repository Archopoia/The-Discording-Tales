Aristese

Sovereign warriors; growth and archives.

In the setting

The Aristese are a people of the Yums origin in The Discording Tales / Des R�cits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Trepharchy (Sovereign Growth).

Relations

Friend: Greyscribes, Navillis. Trusting: Meridians. Neutral: Slaadeans, Tchalkchaïs. Wary: Lake Ylves. Hostile: Great Ylves, Pale Ylves.

Lineages

Monte-Foudres

You Monte-foudres (Mykkên) would have lost your emotions to your muscles, straying through your longest, warlike, rugged transhumance… How many of your nomapalais mercenaries did you lose in those far hostile lands, your sufferings succumbing to betrayals? Hundreds? Thousands? O how your carved, uplifted stature beautifies your darkened fur and honours your lutteurs-cavaliers defending our archives with your pavois swelling with each clash.

  • Physique: More sculpted, taller, with fur of a darker brown.
  • Traits: The Aristese Monte-foudres form the physically strongest tribe, at the cost of empathy worn down by their need to express their power.
  • Traditions: You would spot them among the lutteurs-cavaliers of the moving archives.

Fleuris

And you Fleuris (Hullur), do you truly descend from the lost colony of Uyahel? Are you the descendants of those who carved there the words pointing to the lands where you dwell today? That would explain your artisans' skill: though of a body yet weaker and frizzled as from some forgotten suffering, they linked and aligned their sails, planks and rails to the world's attractive forces to send those Beingsts far away.

  • Physique: Broader stature and curlier fur.
  • Traits: The Fleuris have precise reflexes, with the striking downside of a lack of grace.
  • Traditions: It would be impossible to escape the watch and shots of one of their côchers-de-titans when they wandered with their herds.

Des Souffles

Our dear Des Souffles (Dhrâix), you of sound roundness and light, bushy fur, who too readily intoxicate yourselves on our cattle's secretions until you act in the perverse secrecy of your debauched, conspiratorial, scandalous feasts… Your sommeliers de sucs delight us all, and your buried drums make our mastodons' tubules vibrate to one same rhythm, though inaudible to anyone not possessing them.

  • Physique: Bushier fur and balanced girth, and fur lighter than the other tribes'.
  • Traits: Aristese Des Souffles are resistant and sturdily built through wind and toil, but their imagination and inventive sense seem less fertile.
  • Traditions: Rare are those who could have outwitted a sommelier de sécrétion from the Des Souffles.

Ambrés

Here at last the Ambrés (Sguth), whose eyes of the same colour seem blind while the brilliance of your genius carries us toward wondrous tomorrows. You deny the existence of our darkest generations to justify your own, as your dompteurs-guérisseurs know how to convince, through the musicality of your tromoroches, our faunas and floras to revive each spring.

  • Physique: Almost all have amber eyes and pilosity in a more or less similar tone.
  • Traits: The most lucid Aristese, the Ambrés tend to be more cautious too, even cowardly or indecisive.
  • Traditions: Their musical dompteurs-guérisseurs could convince and regenerate faunas and floras through their instruments.
