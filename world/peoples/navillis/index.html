Peoples · Yômmes

Navillis

YÔMMES Olbophely (Devoted Felicity)

Maritime tribes; boat-gardens, joy.

In the setting

The Navillis are a people of the Yômmes origin in The Discording Tales / Des Récits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Olbophely (Devoted Felicity).

Relations

Friend: Greyscribes. Trusting: Aristese. Welcoming: Tchalkchaïs, Iqqars. Neutral: Slaadeans. Wary: Pale Ylves. Hostile: Great Ylves, Lake Ylves.

Lineages

Carmin

The Carmins (Gweidhel) have a stockier build that gives them strength as well as inflexibility, with dark ruddy to auburn teints and brown eyes. With them you would have claimed to have discovered those living beings in the greatest depths of our oceans, who today power our communications through their lights, smokes and shifting colours that we grow, arrange and send. Everyone thinks that was only an old lie from their buveurs d'histoires with broad stomachs to keep their secrets, and that they simply raised them to those tasks down the ages and generations.

  • Physique: They are naturally even stockier than the norm (Massive), with dark ruddy to auburn teints and brown eyes.
  • Traits: Navillis Carmins are the strongest, and that same strength seems to limit their suppleness and grace.
  • Traditions: They would have buveurs d'histoires with wide knowledge and wide stomachs.

Pétricole

Pétricoles (Galnad) have features carved and tenacious as stone, amber eyes perhaps less reactive though sometimes ranging from blue to brown. With their mangent-tout in courts and kitchens they exposed the medical falsehood of eating our dead bodies. Instead they grasped harmony between beasts and ourselves to meet shared needs, down to lighting the ocean depths.

  • Physique: Broad and carved like rock, usually amber eyes sometimes blue to brown.
  • Traits: Tenacious against their elements, Navillis Pétricoles also seem to have inherited slower reactions.
  • Traditions: Their mangent-tout serve in many courts and kitchens, though always watched.

Perce-Vague

Lastly the slender Perces-vagues (Kallad), creative blue eyes in straw teints, are the least sympathetic - as their past shows when they judged beasts like Navillis and punished them for misdeeds they set up. Their confectionneurs marins can fulfil anyone's desire, even to open a way between anything with their growing bridges across any ground, pit or river.

  • Physique: Somewhat taller and slimmer, blue eyes, straw teints.
  • Traits: Greater determination than the other tribes, from which they also stand apart in common sympathy.
  • Traditions: Their confectionneurs marins can fulfil anyone's desire, whether as an object or by guiding them to safe harbour.
