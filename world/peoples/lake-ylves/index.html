Lineages

Mâche-Troncs

Lake Ylves Mâche-Troncs (Trurhsyôr) are imposing, black and frizzled, with piercing green eyes like the deep waters where they once gnawed tangled trunks. Isolated and overlooked by others, they pursued an ancient war they thought eternal long after others forgot it. Their officiers-jongleurs, masters of martial poetry, one day made their hydrobèle trebuchets dance on their tallest hydropyrgue siege towers far beyond hope, fascinating the other races when they rediscovered them.

  • Physique: Imposing, black frizzled fur, piercing green eyes.
  • Traits: Lake Ylves Mâche-Troncs (Yalnadd'Dald in Noscois) are more solid and enduring, at the cost of less sympathy.
  • Traditions: Their officiers-jongleurs are masters of martial poetry.

Troglodytes

Lake Ylves Troglodytes (Rhaïtyôr) stand out with tall lean frames, thick grey fur, piercing blue or emerald eyes like the secret gleams of their flooded cavern-pillars. There they fought ritual duels where each move of their maîtres chante-contes rang with aquatic echoes. Thus they sang each battle in coded airs whose hydrosèmiques shaped water into speech for other races, like their téléhydrie of canals and valves squeezing water into words.

  • Physique: Tall and lean, thick grey fur, piercing blue or emerald eyes.
  • Traits: Lake Ylves Troglodytes (Yalnadd'Rald in Noscois) have a quicker, keener mind, but duller aura and graces than other Ylves.
  • Traditions: Their maîtres chante-contes led ritual clashes in tune with aquatic echoes.

Danse-Feuilles

Lake Ylves Danse-Feuilles (Lpondyôr) move with fluid grace, bluish fur and piercing green eyes like emeralds lost in the over-woods. Seized long ago by a collective dance frenzy, they were swept into uncontrollable rhythms of leaves in the wind that marked their souls and fused them into a new awareness, forging the first diplo-traumas, rituals able to heal the mind by unsettling it. They developed ascenseurs hydrores to glide through branche-canaux and tronc-canaux, and networks of potamic canals with barges on waters thus steered, carrying goods or allied races through their domains like initiatory paths in the motion of arboricol waters.

  • Physique: Fluid grace, bluish fur, piercing green eyes.
  • Traits: Lake Ylves Danse-Feuilles (Yalnadd'Nold in Noscois) have livelier, brighter grace, but seem physically frailer.
  • Traditions: Their diplo-traumas practise rituals able to heal the mind by unsettling it.
