Slaadeans

BÊSTRES Thrasoniry (Dreaming Audacity)

Bold dreamers; life 2 - 115 years.

In the setting

The Slaadeans are a people of the Bêstres origin in The Discording Tales / Des Récits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Thrasoniry (Dreaming Audacity).

Relations

Trusting: Among themselves. Neutral: All Yômmes, Lake Ylves, Tchalkchaïs. Wary: Great Ylves, Pale Ylves, Iqqars.

Lineages

Sydru-kal

Slaadeans Sydru-kal (Sablevents), slender with ivory skin and rare green, pink or blue feathers and black, brown or green eyes, and the hairier, plumed Slaadeans Wijriol (Chante-neiges) with brown to black skin plus green or amber eyes, would once both have been dug from snowy tombs before reclothing to resume their danced lives. Sydru-kal glisse-souffles would be so dexterous they could float, dancing on winds that cut, notably with pneumopode walkers whose steps beat out heat.

  • Physique (Sydru-kal / Sablevents): Slender ivory skin, rare green, pink or blue feathers, black, brown or green eyes.
  • Traits (Sydru-kal / Sablevents): Slaadean Sablevents have matchless dexterity, but seem physically frailer.
  • Traditions (Sydru-kal / Sablevents): Their glisse-souffles can float dancing on winds that cut.

Wijriol

You would hear the guttural chants of Wijriol hurle-muscs steering snows of scents their voices conjure, even guiding atmonaute boats adrift on temperatures driven to extremes.

  • Physique (Wijriol / Chante-neiges): Hairier and plumed, brown to black skin, green or amber eyes.
  • Traits (Wijriol / Chante-neiges): Slaadean Chante-neiges are more vigorous and hardy, but less agile and dexterous.
  • Traditions (Wijriol / Chante-neiges): Their hurle-muscs steer snows of scents born of their voices.

Hlabayan

Slaadeans Hlabayan (Mangrovians) with fire-red skin and feathers sometimes streaked blue, green or blue eyes, and Slaadeans Felichrem (Rosebrousses) with the same striation on more salmon skin, would have been awakened from the snows by the mysterious sky-tremblings once heard across the desert. Armed with pneumocorax armours and shields whose vapours melt any snow, the reactive, fully shielded Hlabayan mur-murs would have formed the first parois guarding any bastion.

  • Physique (Hlabayan / Mangrovians): Fire-red skin and feathers sometimes streaked blue, green or blue eyes.
  • Traits (Hlabayan / Mangrovians): Slaadean Mangrovians are more perceptive and reactive, but their aura and graces seem duller.
  • Traditions (Hlabayan / Mangrovians): Their mur-murs, reactive and fully shielded, form the first parois guarding any bastion.

Felichrem

Likewise, the Felichrem dompte-rêves, through their réseau téléatmique crossing the dunes while burning them, would have broadcast every habit, desire and need, wild or not, of beings round about through tubed words reaching them all.

  • Physique (Felichrem / Rosebrousses): Same striation on more salmon-toned skin.
  • Traits (Felichrem / Rosebrousses): Slaadean Rosebrousses have a dominating, persuasive mind, but seem less resistant to the elements.
  • Traditions (Felichrem / Rosebrousses): Their dompte-rêves perceive the habits, desires and needs of all beings round about.

Iossolam

Slaadeans Iossolam (of the Oasis), turquoise-skinned with long red-to-green feathers and green, brown or black eyes, would once have farmed thermal oases (heresy!). From there their rils would issue by night to fight those who tried to stop them, freezing them with cryothermic cannons whose blasts pump out all heat, still used by their brûle-sages meditating in the depths of their searing golden oases, where they paint their broad lore on walls thus chilled.

  • Physique: Turquoise skin, long red-to-green feathers, green, brown or black eyes.
  • Traits: Slaadeans of the Oasis are more intelligent and wise, but seem less physically vigorous.
  • Traditions: Their brûle-sages meditate in the depths of their searing golden oases.
