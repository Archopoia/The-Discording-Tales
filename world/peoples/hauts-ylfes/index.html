Lineages

De Lunes

Turned blue by the eight moons and blessed by them in a fertility of spirits, the white or grey eyes of the chasse-richesses Great Ylves de Lune (Yngvyôr) shine at night when they set out eight by eight, sounding their tymbafrons to revulse the unwanted, to welcome the ninth moon from which they would come.

  • Physique: Thinner build, bluish fur, white or grey eyes.
  • Traits: Great Ylves de Lune (Ôbaald'Yrod in Noscois) have great fertility of spirit, in exchange for lesser strength than other Great Ylves.
  • Traditions: Their chasse-richesses would go out in eights and shine on nights of the full moons.

Des Plaines

Pale, thick-furred moines des murmures, Great Ylves des Plaines (Yrmnyôrs) with blue-green eyes, were born from the warlike conquest of their singing statue, and they still sing when they slaughter in peace and harmony through their téléséismes vibrating across the plains, amplifying their words and relaying their hard-won lore where it is deserved.

  • Physique: Very thick-furred and somewhat shorter, white fur, blue or green eyes.
  • Traits: Greater sympathy than the others, yet Great Ylves des Plaines (Öbaald'Eiold in Noscois) seem less sharp of instinct.
  • Traditions: Their moines des murmures would sing to fight in peace and harmony, and also to locate their prey.
