Meridians

YÔMMES Eteathly (Competitive Authenticity)

Prove worth by action; austèles, Labyrinthe.

In the setting

The Meridians are a people of the Yômmes origin in The Discording Tales / Des Récits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Eteathly (Competitive Authenticity).

Relations

Trusting: Among themselves. Welcoming: From other Yômmes. Wary: Toward other Yômmes. Hostile: Yôrres and Bêstres.

Lineages

Sylvains

Slender, fair Sylvains (Tewtehn) with grey eyes would have won animal dexterity and delicacy by sacrificing those monsters in their arboricol sources where their docteurs climb in adhesive gloves and armour vanishes into the water's reflections.

  • Physique: Slimmer than the others, fair teints tending blond, grey eyes.
  • Traits: Meridians Sylvains traded typical strength for dexterity and delicacy.
  • Traditions: A tradition of docteurs healing in and through the liquids of their arboricol sources.

Des Monts

Tall, broad, fat and brown Meridians des Monts (Guthiud) with blue eyes, in their joviality danced prophecies from their fingers on the corpses of their vanquished, to torment storms on their enemies or guide us with coloured smoke. Each danced body would for them be a way to reach ancestors come from the abysses, whom they try to rejoin with crystal chariots that charge from their motion.

  • Physique: Tall and broad or fat, mostly brown fur and blue eyes.
  • Traits: More jovial and pleasant than their cousins, Meridians des Monts have a frailer constitution in return.
  • Traditions: They would dance for hours on their fingers to reveal prophecies or to torment the winds.

Marche-Rêves

Ivory Marche-Rêve (Tkhim-ro) with ice-blue eyes would be of a forgotten blood with unknown script and lost ways, but kept their superior imagination. Through divination with floating, estranging rocks, their spiritual guides, blades cutting through everything, hid or laid bare our words to all during the purge, reading with their fingers the precious shifting textures of those stones.

  • Physique: Ivory fur, ice-blue eyes.
  • Traits: Meridians Marche-Rêve have the highest imagination in their family, yet that seems to distract them until it throws off their sync with the world around them.
  • Traditions: Their spiritual guides could use a floating, estranging rock as a source of divination.
