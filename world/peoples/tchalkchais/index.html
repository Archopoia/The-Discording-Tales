Tchalkchaïs

BEINGSTS Echotysy (Sacrificial Altruism)

Sacrificial altruists; life 10 - 27 years.

In the setting

The Tchalkchaïs are a people of the Beingsts origin in The Discording Tales / Des R�cits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Echotysy (Sacrificial Altruism).

Relations

Trusting: Among themselves. Welcoming: Navillis, Iqqars. Neutral: Aristese, Greyscribes, Meridians, Pale Ylves, Slaadeans. Wary: Great Ylves. Hostile: Lake Ylves.

Lineages

Elesihin

The Tchalkchaïs Elesihin (Sablevents), tall with pistachio skin without contrast, bear piercing amber or green eyes, emblems of unchanging endurance and health. Born under a sky where the stars seemed to align in ancient auguries, they became guardians of the secrets of time; their perce-temps see not only through roches-bleues and desert pink sands, but also the echoes of ages past and future. They developed héliobole catapults, huge engines hurling shot for leagues on heat gathered from the sun, in tune with their knack for reading nature's secret rhythms.

  • Physique: Tall, pistachio skin without contrast, amber or piercing green eyes.
  • Traits: Tchalkchaïs Elesihin (Sand-Guides) have unchanging endurance and health, but seem less charismatic and persuasive.
  • Traditions: Their perce-temps see through roches-bleues and pink desert sands, and the echoes of ages past and future.

Ordol

Tchalkchaïs Ordol (the Blues), turquoise-chitined with sparkling green eyes, are of unbreakable resolve; their green pilosity trembles in the breezes of their lands. Marked by lore from the farthest skies, they often speak of invisible stars that guided their ancestors toward a promised land beyond the horizon. Their ludo-rhéteurs, masters of word and wager, are famed for outrageous stakes - once convincing a whole people to risk fate on a kingdom in one mischievous grain of sand. With navhélion boats whose vast sails catch slanting sun to drive across dunes, the Ordol sail the sands, trailing legends and bold bets.

  • Physique: Turquoise chitin, sparkling green eyes, green pilosity as well.
  • Traits: Tchalkchaïs Ordol are of unbreakable resolve, but seem less agile and dexterous.
  • Traditions: Their ludo-rhéteurs are masters of word and wager, famed for outrageous stakes.

Îohadam

The Tchalkchaïs Îohadam (Sun-kindlers), peach-skinned with amber or green eyes, of medium height, are famed for visionary innovation. Heirs of a glorious age of hallucinated cities, they saw their dearest artefacts scattered in a ruinous assault from beyond the dunes. The labour-rêves, masters of the fleeting, would now grow spectacular mirages that glitter on the horizon themselves. They devised héliergon tools, drawing the sun's energy and the river's water to animate their labours.

  • Physique: Medium height, peach skin, amber or green eyes.
  • Traits: Tchalkchaïs Îohadam (Sun-kindlers) are famed for visionary innovation and wit, but seem less physically hardy.
  • Traditions: Their labour-rêves grow spectacular mirages that glitter on the horizon.

Nuôtarjoy

Tchalkchaïs Nuôtarjoy (Rock-bearers), broad and muscular, wear pistachio chitin marked with fine bluish gills that tremble to the tides of the underground rivers they rule. Once guided by luminous apparitions of strange, ethereal beings crossing their misty marshes, they read those visions as sacred guides toward unreachable architecture. Their tire-rivières, river-tamers, thus built unshakeable citadels on mountains and flots that no invader has reached. With héliocorax armours forged from the fleeting gleam of those spectral visitors, they became a feared power, moving with agility and force along the shores where their master builders still raise mountains of stone and water.

  • Physique: Broad and muscular, pistachio chitin, fine bluish gills.
  • Traits: Tchalkchaïs Nuôtarjoy are powerful and hardy, but seem less perceptive and attentive.
  • Traditions: Their tire-rivières, tamers of currents, build unshakeable citadels on mountains and flots.

Ikrayad

Tchalkchaïs Ikrayad (Short-dunes), graceful bodies of sand hue, black or green eyes bright in gloom, move in silence with extreme delicacy, leaving floating mirages like untouchable shadows. Ancient adepts of a secret order from dunes of heady dust, they became masters of concealment and ethereal manoeuvre, tracking targets with bottomless loyalty. Their marche-ombres would be the source of every mirage that trails behind them while they vanish into it, while their réseaux téléhéliques - assemblies of mirrors that bend sun or moonbeams - let them flash coded messages in an instant.

  • Physique: Graceful sand-hued bodies, black or green eyes bright in gloom.
  • Traits: Tchalkchaïs Ikrayad are extremely agile and discreet, masters of concealment, but less hardy against frontal blows.
  • Traditions: Their marche-ombres would be the source of every mirage that trails behind them while they vanish within it.
