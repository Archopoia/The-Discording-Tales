Lineages

Collinicoles

The Pale Ylves Collinicoles (Skanyôr), stronger still when they wear their cratocorax lever-armours that boost their strength and means, have sandy frizzled fur and deep brown eyes, sometimes green or a rare blue like their cliffs. Born in the mountains above the hills, inside their mastodons they held their vast fairs; their éleveurs-prêteurs thus built cattle kingdoms, mechanically encoding their dealings in logomates through the coin they created, the Cordon d'Or - which financed many wars to come.

  • Physique: Stronger and more imposing, sandy frizzled fur, deep brown eyes sometimes blue or green.
  • Traits: Pale Ylves Collinicoles (Mountaineers) (Yalnadd'Nädim in Noscois) are hardy in body and against mental assault, but that says nothing of their graces, far lower than most Ylves'.
  • Traditions: They invented coin and keep a tradition of éleveurs-prêteurs.

Chromatiques

The Pale Ylves Chromatiques (Palasgyôr) stand out with bluish fur and piercing azure eyes, emblems of radiant health. Former masters of lands once decorated with their gigantic symbols on the hill soils that changed colour with the seasons, they did not hesitate to fragment and reshape their own works there to set the protective gems that adorned them. Their gemmologistes-serruriers drive rolling strongboxes along those symbol-paths, announcing and escorting crossings with their network of téléstoles towers with flags over the hills, flags visible for leagues, and mechanical télécordion networks enabling secret exchanges between their corded cities.

  • Physique: Wider girth, bluish fur, piercing azure eyes.
  • Traits: Radiant health and resistance to the elements, yet Pale Ylves Chromatiques (Yalnadd'Eiss in Noscois) seem far less observant and attentive than their kin.
  • Traditions: Their gemmologistes-serruriers drive rolling strongboxes and are famed for protective gems.

Pastoraux

Pale Ylves Pastoraux (Mindyôr), nasturtium-pink fur and green eyes like the meadows they cherish, watch every life around them closely, reading the urges of their dangerous herds. They would have returned from a past that once petrified them like an army, issuing from a great nécribunal once warded by liquid deadly gold. From the past they brought hypsébole catapults that launch archers-guetteurs in cushioned eggs toward trampled pastures, and tensodrome rails whose ramps in one rush send coaches of cattle and supplies through the trees to their cities.

  • Physique: Tall but lean, nasturtium-pink fur, green eyes like their meadows.
  • Traits: Pale Ylves Pastoraux (Yalnadd'Imeld in Noscois) have sharper instinct than their kin, but seem less resistant to physical blows.
  • Traditions: Their archers-guetteurs are launched from catapults in cushioned eggs toward pastures they watch.
