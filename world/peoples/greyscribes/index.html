Greyscribes

YUMS Mignusophy (Nested Wisdom)

Nested sages; mangroves and truths.

In the setting

The Greyscribes are a people of the Yums origin in The Discording Tales / Des R�cits Discordants - an ethno-science-fantasy world without humans. Their racial morality is framed as Mignusophy (Nested Wisdom).

Relations

Friend: Navillis. Trusting: Aristese. Welcoming: Meridians. Neutral: Slaadeans, Tchalkchaïs, Iqqars. Wary: Pale Ylves. Hostile: Great Ylves, Lake Ylves.

Lineages

Arboricoles

The first shamans of our Arboricoles (B'hel), with colours of vivid fire pierced by amber or blue eyes, whose bulk does not blunt their dexterity, are said all to have died, leaving bodies without eyes, tongues or brows in the mist-haunted passes of Izkaraï. From then on they invented mount-rails drawing first the gear of their démineurs-déments, who there defuse exponential growth bubbles from those deadly mists.

  • Physique: They are heavier, wearing colours of vivid fire with amber or blue eyes.
  • Traits: Greater speed and precision than the others, but in return they are less strong.
  • Traditions: It would be impossible to run down their caravaniers d'or-mou, who could navigate even upside down on faces, hide everywhere and survive the strangling mists.

Saute-Brumes

Shamans of the Saute-Brumes (Sklowot), with the same eyes but more graceful, taller, with a redder, lighter teint than the others, would have seen from their tallest tree a city of light in the sky before it vanished for all. Before that, all made pilgrimage to that tree with caravaniers whose mastodons were raised in and born of gold, navigating even upside down on faces, travelling hidden, surviving their strangling mists.

  • Physique: Slimmer and taller, a redder, lighter teint than the others, amber or blue eyes.
  • Traits: With discreet, delicate steps from prudence or fear, Greyscribe Saute-Brumes are more graceful than their kin, but that caution may cost them vitality.
  • Traditions: You would know their démineurs-déments when they busy themselves defusing exponential growth bubbles from their mists.

Limnicoles

In auburn teints and blue eyes, the Limnicoles (Prus'sg), nearly vanished - less lucid but more ingenious - would have survived in secret, flourishing cities like paradise within the chasms below us. There their inventeurs-enlisés feed on mists digested through the mud that grips them to produce their inventions, such as aerial or aquatic physiosèmie that reacts to certain things to shift colour or texture and reveal its secrets.

  • Physique: Auburn and other deep red teints, usually blue eyes.
  • Traits: Greyscribe Limnicoles have keen instinct but seem less ingenious than most of their kin.
  • Traditions: It is said their inventeurs-enlisés feed on mists digested through the gripping mud to fuel their great ingenuity.

D'Eau

Brown with blue eyes, all shaved and braided the same way, those of Water (Wendd) are welcoming and warm as long as they need no boldness, as when their sophisto-parfumeurs raised these volatiles that recognise any marked individual or thing and spoke with them through special waters for study or politics. Thus they convinced some to treachery by opening borders to armies, treasurers to drain wealth, or spread rumours that once drove their foes to mass suicide.

  • Physique: Medium height and bulk for a Greyscribe, brown fur, blue eyes.
  • Traits: Greyscribes of Water are welcoming and warm as long as it costs nothing in daring.
  • Traditions: Many sophisto-parfumeurs would roam the waters, convincing whoever interests them for their studies, or be hired to shield against that.
