**THE DISCORDING TALES**

**The ethno-science-fantasy role-playing game**

*Where everything is foreign, unstable and dangerous - and where the extreme becomes coherent, playable, alive.*

---

**Why this game exists**

I wrote **The Discording Tales** so that, through my creativity, players would collide with situations, cultures and environments they thought impossible - and make them possible. By taking the most extreme, opposed and incongruous things, I make them **coherent**, then **interactive**.

This is not one more setting. It is a **genre** proposition: **BRIGHTSTRIFE** (*Luminous Struggle*) - an inversion of dystopia without being utopia through a twist: here, the universe is a **utopia of extremes**: struggle is fundamentally good; conflict is beautiful, virtuous, necessary. The "evil" is whatever reduces intensity through inaction, nihilism, apathy. A **Nietzschean** fantasy - which, to my knowledge, has never yet been done in role-playing history.

**HOOK**

**The Discording Tales** is an anthropological exploration role-playing game where you survive, clash and transcend in a world **without humans**, through cultures, environments and extreme moralities you thought impossible - until the system makes them **coherent and interactive**. Every adventure is a social, mental or physical challenge. **Failure forges the hero**: that is how you progress here.

*In a distant future where humanity has withdrawn, humanoid peoples collide on a forgotten Earth - and you are the local who grew up in a blind spot and must understand to survive.*

**In one sentence**

An RPG where every failure brings you closer to mastery, where magic is born from the pain and relief of a goddess braided by the forces of the world, and where your values determine your Insanity - in a universe where ten peoples see each other as tools, resources, food or mad.

**What the player lives**

- **Discover** exotic ways of life and survive in them as a local - a *local in a strange land*.
- **Face** fanatical moralities without flattening them into universal "good" or "evil".
- **Progress** through suffering and failure, seeking ever more painful challenges.
- **Create** freely: no classes, no fixed gifts - total permissiveness through real consequences.

**Tone:** weird, conflictual, survivalist, deep. **Scale:** from one-shot to Westmarch campaign.


**THE SETTING: COSMOS & SECRET**

**There is only discord**

Under our feet, in the blossoming depths of a forgotten Earth, **there is only discord**.

In a far, far future, our descendants the posthuman **Aïars** - masters of crystalline technomagic - vanished into the depths. Their legendary ruins dot a transformed Earth. **There are no more humans** - or almost: traces, relics, technologies frozen in ethereal magic. A strong and rare argument: a fantasy universe **without humans**, where current peoples derive from genetic, mythic and selective origins operated by the Aïars before their withdrawal.

**Ten peoples, three origins**

The world is inhabited by **ten peoples** in three radically opposed origins:


|             |                                                  |                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| **Origin**  | **Peoples**                                      | **Fantasy correspondence**             |
| **Yômmes**  | Aristese, Greyscribes, Navillis, Meridians         | Small races / hybrid humanoids         |
| **Yôrres**  | Great Ylves, Pale Ylves, Lake Ylves, Iqqars        | Elves / evolved humanoids              |
| **Bêstres** | Slaadeans, Tchalkchaïs                            | Animal races / evolved creatures       |


Far from Tolkien archetypes: **divergent** fundamental principles, inherited unconscious moralities, cultures in permanent tension. Close to *Talislanta*, *Yoon Suin* or *Empire of the Petal Throne* for exotism; to *Star Wars* × *Dark Crystal* × *Warhammer* for baroque wonder **within** conflict.

**Plot twist: Cytocosmism**

All believe they live in a **concave universe** - the cosmos wrapped in rock, the ground beneath their feet perhaps endless - and so it is the earthly depths that become the cosmic far-west. Cytocosmism is possibly a perceptual illusion - a "pincushion" vision. This reversal of perspective explains why discovering the world is an adventure in itself.

**Rilie: magic-physics**

Cords braided from two threads, knotted in every element; the whole universe born from one continuous cord, whose knots are the **Rils**. **Rilic** magic is not a bolt-on. It is **rational animism**: gravity, light, emotion and transmutation are interactions with the world's fundamental forms (Rils), orchestrated by four cosmic forces - electromagnetism, gravity, light, transmutation - embodied by the tetrarchs of **HISM**.

At the centre: **Ô**, world-goddess, universal braided cord, constantly tugged between **pain** and **relief** by the cosmic forces. No spell lists: **Rilical Honorations**, rituals, manipulations of the world's physics. Magic is explained, integrated, playable.

**THE SETTING: CONFLICT, DAILY LIFE & ANCHORING**

**Mental images - three peoples, three worlds**

**The Aristese** (Yômmes) - *Trepharchy*, sovereign growth: On their **mastodons**, giant fur starfish, they dominate from the backs of **high flowering plateaus** in **palace-sculptures** covered with statues. Their **nomadic library** hoards all knowledge; they see Bêstres as wild beasts. Pastoralism, expansion of knowledge, law proclaimed over the dominated.

**The Slaadeans** (Bêstres) - *Thrasony*, dreamer's audacity: **Nomadic caravans** with pointed solar metal roofs, reflecting light in **cold snowed dunes** pierced by **hot-water springs** and **thermal mangroves**. Hunter-gatherers; society founded on **strength** and its value. They see Aristese as **tools**, Great Ylves as **incomprehensible aliens**.

**The Great Ylves** (Yôrres) - *Edapoesy*, creative competence: Hierarchical cannibals - they see Slaadeans as **skins to extract** for their seats, their **bones** as alchemical ingredients; Aristese as **food to hunt**.

Each interprets the other: tool, resource, food, mad, animal. **This is the heart of the game**: anthropological exploration through culture shock - where each needs their Insanities not only to survive, but to flourish there.

**Structural conflicts**

- **Economic:** hunter-gatherers (Slaadeans) vs pastoralism (Aristese) vs agriculture (Lake Ylves).
- **Cultural:** nomads vs sedentary / semi-sedentary; **techno-traditionalism** - the opposition of **People** (society, customs) and **Things** (nature, technology).
- **Religious:** Heptahalie vs Kaïaste Oratory vs Aristese Nomê vs Ylfe Hahalumullio - as many incompatible truths.

**Living currency**

Wealth is not abstract:

- **Livestock herds** - living currency on four legs with needs to manage.
- **Luminous hides** - fuel producing light.
- **Golden cords** - power and prestige materialised in beauty.

The richer you are, the harder travel becomes: you move with your living capital.

**Play region: Iäoduneï**

Default gateway - **~200,000 souls** over **232,000 km²**: snowed dunes, hot waters, central mountains, **Hylotherm Mangroves** (carmine trees, **Nomachome** caravan-villages). This is not the world's only plateau: it is the **door** to other extreme environments, each with its peoples, resources, conflicts.

**THE DISCORDING SYSTEM (I): PHILOSOPHY & STRUCTURE**

**Rules Heavy, Procedures Light**

**Philosophy:** extreme permissiveness in a reimagined *Pathfinder* vein, **clear mathematical consequences** for every choice, BUT **without** reference tables or hollow improvisation. Between simplified and complex play: your decisions **matter**, without concocting abstract puzzles.

- **No classes.** No fixed gifts.
- **Everything is possible from the start** - the higher you aim, the more complexity emerges **elegantly**.
- **Free-forming**: modular rule blocks, independent, optional - the character unfolds over sessions, as does the game's complexity.
- **Formulas with few variables** rather than large tables - elegant design between complexity and simplicity.

*For those tired of abstract, confusing restrictions, but who want their decisions to have mechanical impact rather than simple wishes.*

**The Discording Dice (dD) - Chance, clusters and Levels**

At the heart of the **Discording System**, there is a three-faced die - **+**, **−**, **0** - called **dD** (*discording dice*). You can roll them as-is, or simulate them with a classic d6 (1-2 = −, 3-4 = 0, 5-6 = +). The result of a handful of dD is not a sum: it is the **discord** between opposing forces - the number of **+** faces minus the number of **−** faces. A roll can thus swing from **−5 to +5** on a base of five dice, with **0** as unstable balance between two camps. This quantified conflict, from −X to +X through zero, gives the system its name: every roll is a small negotiation between what pushes and what restrains.

Around it forms a **dice cluster** - all the dD you roll to resolve an action. To these five dice are added **Degrees** from your Skills (positive dice) or Sufferings (negative dice), plus a few other factors. A skilled, little-afflicted character can thus throw **8, 10, 13 dD or more** on a decisive action while keeping only the 5 highest or lowest dice depending on the final sign of the extra Dice total; a wounded, exhausted one sees their cluster swell toward the negative, accumulating misfortune rather than mastery.

To the discord of the five retained dice is then added the **Level (Niv)**: an integer that **shifts** the whole curve of possible results. Niv comes mainly from your Aptitude and modifiers (tool, situation, title, etc.). With Niv +3 and a 5 dD Chance, your final results run from −2 to +8 rather than −5 to +5 - but the ceiling stays disciplined: the total never exceeds the Aptitude's **Niv + 5**. Niv and Degrees can also convert into each other when you need speed at the table (1-2 Dice ≈ 1 Niv, 3-5 Dice ≈ 2 Niv, and so on). In practice, a roll reads thus: **Niv + result of the five dD retained in the cluster**.

Facing an action, the Revealer sets a **Trial Level** - difficulty, from "ridiculously easy" (−5N) to "matter of legends" (+10N and beyond). Success if your final result meets or beats the Trial Level; failure otherwise - and it is the severity of failure that starts the progression engine.

**The 8 Columns - one rule, eight applications**

The whole system rests on **one logic**, repeated eight times like eight parallel columns. Each column starts from an **Attribute** (Strength, Agility, Dexterity, Vigor, Empathy, Perception, Creativity, Willpower), derives an **Aptitude**, attaches a **Suffering** that erodes rolls as pain accumulates, opens **three Action Verbs** - active ways to interact with the world - and, under each verb, **three Skills** the character **discovers over play** rather than choosing all at creation. Eight Attributes, eight Aptitudes, twenty-four Actions, **seventy-two Skills**: the depth is there, but it unfolds progressively, avoiding choice paralysis while rewarding exploration.

Each Aptitude is also a **conflict type** - a way to obtain the coveted Object or cross the Obstacle blocking the road - and a **Caste**, an archetype of role in the world as at the table:


|                |              |                |           |
| -------------- | ------------ | -------------- | --------- |
| **Aptitude**   | **Conflict** | **Suffering**  | **Caste** |
| **Power**      | Battle       | Wounds         | Warriors  |
| **Ease**       | Infiltration | Bonds          | Rovers    |
| **Precision**  | Craft        | Fatigues       | Artisans  |
| **Athletics**  | Feat         | Deprivations   | Workers   |
| **Charisma**   | Corruption   | Addictions     | Aides     |
| **Detection**  | Investigation| Diseases       | Merchants |
| **Reflection** | Enigma       | Insanities     | Scholars  |
| **Domination** | Debate       | Grudges        | Priests   |


The warrior who smashes a convoy, the rover who steals it unseen, the artisan who lures the guards, the worker who crosses a chasm, the aide who calms a caravan, the merchant who negotiates peace, the scholar who solves the impossible, the priest who imposes their law: **eight paths of equal depth**, eight ways to be a hero. There is no "secondary" path in the system.

This structure does not stop at the character. It echoes in the cosmic - **eight seasons**, **eight moons**, **eight Rildeed-astral**, eight rilic movements - as if the whole universe had been folded to the same pattern. **One column, one rule; eight columns, the whole game.**

**Experience through failure**

In *The Discording Tales*, failure is not silent punishment: it is the main path of learning. Whenever you fail a **Possible Trial** with an already revealed Skill, you inscribe a **Mark** in that Skill - a trace of what pain or powerlessness taught you. A **critical failure** (the unlucky Astragale) carves **five Marks** at once. Ten Marks - minus your Eternal Marks, those of Symbol, education or passion - and the Skill is **Tested**: ready to tip.

**Realisation** is the moment of epiphany: you erase your ordinary Marks, gain **+1 Die** in the Skill, distribute free Marks in neighbouring Skills (by proximity in the tree of Actions and Aptitudes), and raise by one point the **Attribute** carrying that path. You can also raise a specialised **Mastery**, or cross a Skill **Level** that unlocks a **Discovery** proper to that path. Thus, the more you expose yourself to the risk of failing, the more your character specialises, deepens and diverges - in accord with Brightstrife: struggle, even failed, brings you closer to what you may become.

**THE DISCORDING SYSTEM (II): SUFFERING, COMBAT, RILIE & SANDBOX**

**The 8 environmental Condition axes - the Revealer's tool**

Every milieu to cross - snowed dune, zoion gallery, Aïar ruin, thermal mangrove - can be described along **eight Condition axes**, mapped to the same logic as the character's eight columns. Each axis opposes two **extremes** of one magnitude: **Pressure** runs from **crushing** to **weightlessness**; **Temperature** from **cold** to **heat**; **Luminance** from **darkness** to blinding **brightness**; **Radiance** from **irriliation** (that rilic order overloading and complexifying, proper to depths and Aïar artefacts) to solar **irradiation**; and so on for **Flow** (bogging / sweeping away), **Humidity** (damp / arid), **Terrain** (slips / steep climbs) and **Breathing** (suffocation / gusting wind).

These Conditions modify rolls within each crossed milieu, **testing** equipment and character **habituation**. On failure, they inflict **Suffering Dice** to resist - generally those tied to the axis. Fur protects from cold but traps heat; a tool solving one extreme tends to expose you to the other: travel becomes a living equipment puzzle.

Thus for the Revealer (GM), **Sufferings and Conditions** form a complementary pair of tools: the former follow the character (wounds, Insanities, Grudges), the latter define the **place** (dust storm, asphyxiating air, disorienting weightlessness). Together they build environments and situations of rare **mechanical precision** - extreme challenges that stay readable, stackable, and narratively explosive.

**The 8 Sufferings**

**Sufferings** are the **inner** counterpart to outer Conditions: Wounds, Fatigues, Bonds, Deprivations, Addictions, Diseases, Insanities, Grudges - each tied to an Attribute, each applied as **Negative Dice** on concerned roll clusters.

When milieu Conditions and Trial failures accumulate into these Suffering Dice - and the character approaches **global thresholds** where body and mind give way:


|                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rage** - acting rationally becomes a Trial in itself; instinct threatens to take the wheel to flee or destroy what caused it beyond the player's hands                       |
| **Blackout** - physical or mental unconsciousness, where the character slips from the player's hands                                                                         |
| **Defeated** - coma, irreversible Insanity, loss of the individual as the Awakener inspiring them knew them                                                                 |


The system does not condemn for all that: you can **guide** your Rage in a flash of lucidity, or - to **convert** suffering, temporarily inverting negative Dice into desperate strength.

**Combat in Clins - without turns**

Combat, in *The Discording Tales*, is the quintessence of **Brightstrife**: the moment when each person's discording tales - are finally measured in action. There is no "turn 3". There is no waiting for your place in initiative. Time **blinks**: a **Clin** equals one third of a second, the length of a blink, and for **all** combatants at once the clock runs - "-1", "-2", "-3"… Each can **always react** to what reaches them - no one is ever spectator to another's turn.

Even before the first blade rises, each rolls their **Alert Clin**. While the general Clin has not reached your Alert Clin, you are caught off guard - but declaration order is only a tactical advantage to anticipate the other, not a pause in the world.

What replaces the turn is **Proactions**: actions requiring several Clins in a row - string a bow, charge a melee blow, etc. You **declare** from the start how many Clins your action will last and at which Clin it will resolve (if you begin a four-Clin attack at Clin −2, you note it lands at Clin −6). You can interrupt and resume from the start of the step begun if the situation shifts - flexibility is total, but memory of what you committed stays at the table. On the **last Clin** of your attack Proaction, the weapon roll falls: damage, success to spend on location or manoeuvres, and whatever the dice cluster decided.

While your Proaction stretches, the enemy - and you - can **React** at each Clin: **block** (absorb at the weapon's price), **parry** (redirect and shorten your next action), **dodge** (leave the trajectory, recover a Clin or a space). Reactions culminate on the **last Clin** of the enemy Proaction, in a roll already burdened by **Shake**. For every Reaction, every abrupt move taken **inside** a Proaction - accumulates as **Shake Niv**, which **subtracts** from all your rolls that Clin. The audacious fighter who wants to do everything at once pays dearly in posture: they stay hittable.  
This is where the system meets the game's creative, conflictual philosophy: **at every instant, we estimate the amount of risk we wish to undertake, and declare it**.

And combat does not belong to warriors alone. **Action Potentials** - dice spent on each **Verb** in Proaction - exist for **all** Aptitudes: *Convince* to turn a target, *Captivate* to rally neutrals, *Conceive* / *Acclimate* to reveal flaws and terrain, *Discern* / *Detect* to read Sufferings and Shake, *Endure* to temper Rage and unconsciousness, *Traverse* / *Exert* to hold the line. **Eight paths, eight ways to be decisive at the same Clin** - charisma, reflection and detection weigh as much as the sword, because in a world where conflict is virtue, every Aptitude of every character must count there.

**Group atmosphere**

The **collective weighs mechanically** on every roll - cohesion, shared successes and failures, Group Roll, Audacity Roll. The individual can take everything on themselves, but the group exists in the numbers.

**Rilie - entirely novel**

- **Freeforming** magic - no spell lists.
- **Rilical Honorations** - rituals of interaction with the Rils.
- **Playable** cosmology: HISM forces, Ô, the cords, the sacrifices.
- Design inspirations: *Ars Magica* (free magic), own cosmology integrated into the setting.

**Mechanical perspectivism**

**Morality** (inherited from People), **Motivation**, **Personality**, **Sociability**, **Disposition**, **Passion** - roleplay is not cosmetic:

- Your **Values** heal your **Insanities**.
- Your **Traits** heal your **Grudges**.
- Eight discording perspectives (Success, Faith, Generosity, Survival…) plus those of your People and social role.

Reward for **embodying** visions foreign to the player - and making them live mechanically.

**Living economy & reimagined Westmarches**

- **Livestock-currency**, **light-silver**, **golden cords**.
- **Labours** (8 h), **Awake / Asleep Time**, **Journeys** with structured encounters.
- **Inspired goals**, strongholds, crafting - world logic that **solves** classic sandbox problems (*Forbidden Lands*, *Ultraviolet Grassland*, *His Majesty the Worm* - reimagined, unified).

**Target audience**

Demanding players - close to *Burning Wheel*, *World of Darkness*, *Pathfinder*, *Ars Magica* - who want **maximum creativity** with real consequences, without reference tables.
