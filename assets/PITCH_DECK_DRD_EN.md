**THE DISCORDING TALES**

**The ethno-science-fantasy role-playing game**

*Where everything is foreign, unstable and dangerous - and where the extreme becomes coherent, playable, alive.*

**The Discording Tales** is an anthropological exploration where you survive, clash and transcend in a world **without humans**, through cultures, environments and extreme moralities you thought impossible - until the system makes them **coherent and interactive**. Every adventure is a social, mental or physical challenge. **Failure forges the hero**: that is how you progress here.

*In a distant future where humanity has withdrawn, humanoid peoples collide on a forgotten Earth - and you are the local who grew up in a blind spot and must learn and understand to survive.*

**The Setting**

Under our feet, in the blossoming depths of a forgotten Earth, **there is only discord**.

In a far, far future, our descendants the posthuman **Aïars** - masters of crystalline technomagic, Rilie - vanished into the depths. Their legendary ruins dot a world inhabited by **ten peoples** in three origins: **Yômmes**, **Yôrres**, **Bêstres** - far from Tolkien archetypes; here, a **Nietzschean fantasy** of divergent principles, close to *Talislanta*, *Yoon Suin* or *Empire of the Petal Throne* for exotism, to *Star Wars* × *Dark Crystal* × *Warhammer* for baroque wonder in conflict.

**Plot twist:** all believe they live in a **concave universe** (Cytocosmism), the cosmos wrapped in rock - and so it is the earthly depths that become the cosmic far-west. **Rilic** magic follows: rational animism where gravity, light and emotion are one braided universal cord, none other than the goddess **Ô** herself, constantly tugged between pain and relief by the cosmic forces behind real physics and magic, being one and the same thing in the end.

**Mental images:** **Aristese** on their mastodons, palace-sculptures on flowering plateaus, nomadic libraries hoarding all knowledge - seeing Bêstres as beasts; **Slaadeans** in caravans with solar metal roofs, hunter-gatherers in snowed dunes and hot-water mangroves - seeing Aristese as tools; hierarchical cannibal **Great Ylves** - seeing some as food, others as resources. Pastoralism versus hunting; People versus Things. Wealth on **four legs** or **shining with light**: herds of livestock and golden cords.

Scale: **Intimate culture shock** opening onto an epic quest of transcendence. A *local in a strange land.*  
Default play region: **Iäoduneï** - central gateway among other extreme environments.

**Tone:** weird, conflictual, survivalist, deep. **Playable** from one-shot to Westmarch campaign.

**The Discording System**

**Philosophy:** *Rules Heavy, Procedures Light* - extreme permissiveness in a reimagined Pathfinder vein, clear mathematical consequences for every choice, **without** reference tables or hollow improvisation. Between simplified and complex play: your decisions **matter**, without abstract puzzles. No classes, no fixed gifts - everything is possible from the start; the higher you aim, the more complexity emerges **elegantly**.

**Five mechanical pillars:**

- **The 8 Columns** - Attributes, Aptitudes, Sufferings, Experience, Conflicts: one rule repeated eight times (8 × 3 Action Verbs × 3 Skills = **72 Skills**). Eight player archetypes - warrior, thief, artisan, priest… - and eight conflict types of **equal depth** (Battle, Investigation, Debate, Infiltration…). Pool of 5 simple Dice (+ / 0 / -) + extra negative/positive Dice + Modifier.
- **Suffering, Rage & real time** - Failure → Marks → progression. Eight Sufferings lead to **Rage**, physical and mental **Blackout**, and finally **Defeat**. Combat runs in **Clins** (1/3 second), **with no turns** - simultaneous and reactive at every instant. **Group atmosphere**: the collective weighs mechanically on every roll although the individual can take everything on themselves.
- **Rilie** - Freeforming magic without spell lists; Rilical Honorations integrating the universe's fundamental physics and the cosmology of cultural animism present there. Entirely novel.
- **Mechanical perspectivism** - Morality, Motivation, Personality, Traits: roleplay as a sharp alternative to **heal** Insanities and Grudges, rewarding the player for embodying foreign visions.
- **Living economy & Westmarches** - Livestock-currency, light-silver, golden cords; inspired goals accumulating off-session resources - world logic solving sandbox problems.

**Target audience:** demanding players, lovers of dice and depth - close to *Burning Wheel*, *World of Darkness*, *Pathfinder*, *Ars Magica* - who want **maximum creativity** with real consequences, reimagined to play without reference tables.

*The system puts the player face to face with otherness: to understand a culture is to accept its rules - mechanical and moral.*

**Planned format:** core book + introduction booklet + **PDF**; paperback / hardcover editions - flexible to your catalogue. Solid manuscript; remaining work is mainly additional content (bestiary, items, scenarios) and refinement through playtests - **ready for strong editorial collaboration**.
