About us

HdP

Hugues de Pingon

Creator of the universe · Creative Director & Lead Game Designer

Creator of The Discording Tales universe and Creative Director of the project. He leads creative direction, game design, gameplay systems, narrative, and the overall coherence of the transmedia world. By day he is Lead Game Designer on The Wayward Realms, developed with the co-creators of The Elder Scrolls series.
