Hugues de Pingon

Creator of the universe · Creative Director & Lead Game Designer

Creator of The Discording Tales universe and Creative Director of the project. Trained in anthropology, biology and neuroscience, then in philosophy and ethics, he built the world so that ideas could become cultures, environments, moral systems and myths rather than remain arguments. He leads creative direction, game design, gameplay systems, narrative, and the overall coherence of the transmedia world. By day he is Lead Game Designer on The Wayward Realms, the spiritual successor of TESII: Daggerfall, led by Ted Peterson and Julian LeFay, the founding fathers of the Elder Scrolls series.

Archopoia

sovereign creativity

01 - Total Freedom

No sacred cows. No creative taboos.

Every idea deserves to be heard before it is judged. We encourage disagreement, criticism, experimentation, and even ideas that may initially seem strange, uncomfortable, politically incorrect, or impossible.

We do not censor creativity to protect feelings. We practice what we call rational dissent: speak directly, challenge ideas openly, and argue for what you believe will make the game better.

No backroom politics. No passive-aggressive silence. No criticism whispered behind someone's back.

The only question that ultimately matters is simple:

Does it make the game better?

This freedom exists for the work, not as an excuse for ego, hostility, or gratuitous provocation.

02 - God Product

The game comes first.

There is only one ego here that matters: the game's.

Personal pride has no authority over the work. Ideas are not defended because they belong to someone. They are defended because they deserve to survive.

Criticism is welcomed when it serves the product. Great ideas are strengthened. Bad ideas are abandoned. Good ideas are transformed.

We are not here to win arguments.

We are here to make something worth playing.

03 - Action over Words

We value what gets done.

Not titles. Not promises. Not corporate language. Not motivational speeches.

Progress is measured in what we actually create: the quality of the work, the consistency of the output, and the ability to turn ideas into reality.

We believe creative freedom means little without discipline.

So we work.

We build. We test. We break things. We rebuild them.

And, occasionally, we laugh at the absurd amount of suffering we voluntarily inflict upon ourselves for the love of art.

Ideas are cheap. Creation is not.

04 - Wild Creativity

Ordered chaos.

We want to make things that could not have been made by committee.

We push ideas as far as they can go: strange, beautiful, unsettling, humorous, extreme, poetic, contradictory. We would rather make something memorable than something merely safe.

That does not mean being provocative for its own sake.

Controversy without purpose is cheap. Originality is not.

If an idea shocks, it should be because it serves the world, the story, the gameplay, or the artistic vision.

Our ambition is not to offend.

It is to create something people have never quite experienced before.

Sovereign Creativity

These principles are deliberately anti-corporate.

We are not trying to recreate the machinery of a large studio on a smaller budget. We are trying to preserve what large creative organizations so often lose:

freedom, obsession, responsibility, and the ability to follow an idea wherever it leads.

Archopoia is the sovereignty of the creator over the creation.

No HR-approved creativity.
No committee-designed worlds.
No focus-grouped imagination.

Just a group of people, somewhere on the internet, obsessively trying to make something extraordinary.

The game is the boss.
