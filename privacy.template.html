Joining the Outpost

If you type an email and press Subscribe, we save four things:

  • the address you typed
  • whether the page was in English or French
  • which page you used: the home page, the pitch, or the video game page
  • the date and time

We use that list for one purpose: to write when Kickstarter opens for The Discording Tales. The launch will include the video game and, on higher tiers, the tabletop role-playing game as a PDF, in early access. We also write for rare news of that same kind. We do not sell the list. We do not pass it to advertisers, other studios, or Kickstarter.

The list is a private spreadsheet at Google. Our own server, hosted by Render, writes each new row. You are asked because you pressed Subscribe. You can take that back at any time, and we will delete the row.

We keep the address until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer send launch news.

Writing to us

The contact form opens a draft in your own email app. The message is not stored on this website. It arrives like any letter you send to thediscordingtales@gmail.com. Sending a question does not add you to the Outpost list.

The Play page

If you talk to the game master, what you type is sent to our server so it can answer. If a character sheet is open, a snapshot of that sheet is sent with the message. The conversation also stays in that browser tab until you close it.

Do not type passwords, home addresses, or other real-world secrets into the game. Those messages are not added to the Outpost list. The server may pass the text to an AI service in order to write the reply.

What stays on your device

There is no advertising and no analytics tracker. The site remembers, in your browser only:

  • the language you chose
  • whether you already came through the entrance, so it does not play again for a few minutes
  • whether archived sections are shown

The music player is SoundCloud’s. If it loads, SoundCloud may set its own cookies. Two typefaces are requested from fonts.wp.com (Automattic). That request includes the usual technical address of your connection, as loading any remote file does. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Archopoia OÜ is in Estonia, in the European Union. Google, Render, SoundCloud, Automattic, and GitHub may process that technical traffic in the United States or elsewhere. Where the law requires a safeguard, those providers use their standard contract clauses or an adequacy decision.

Your choices

Email thediscordingtales@gmail.com. You can ask to see what we hold, correct it, delete it, limit it, or receive a copy. Asking to delete your Outpost address stops the launch letters.

You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, Estonia, aki.ee. If you live elsewhere in the European Union, you may contact your local authority as well.

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change with it.
