What is ethno-science-fantasy?

A short definition for players, GMs, and searchers who want something stranger than recycled high fantasy - and more coherent than a mood board.

Definition

Ethno-science-fantasy is fantasy built around cultures first: peoples with their own moralities, bodies, and technologies of the strange, set in a cosmos that behaves like myth and physics at once. In The Discording Tales / Des R�cits Discordants, there are no humans. You play inside alien needs, desires, and wills that only make sense when the rules and the lore lock together.

How it differs from high fantasy

High fantasy often centers a human (or human-adjacent) moral default and a familiar medieval toolkit. Ethno-science-fantasy starts from other peoples - Yums, Yors, Beingsts and their nations - and asks what growth, wisdom, joy, or competence mean when the body and the Ril are not yours.

How it differs from soft science fantasy

Science fantasy can mean lasers on dragons. Here the "science" is diegetic: crystal forests where matter inverts, abundances and festivals named as forces, artefacts and orders that structure play. The wonder is ethnographic and systemic, not only spectacle.
