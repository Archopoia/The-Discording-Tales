The game

The Discording Tales is an ethno-science-fantasy action RPG. You survive, clash, and go further in a world without humans, through cultures, places, and moralities that feel impossible until the rules make them coherent and playable. Every adventure is a social, mental, or physical challenge. Failure forges the hero. That is how you grow.

In a far future, humanity has withdrawn. Ten peoples, in three origins, collide on a forgotten Earth, and you are the local who grew up in a blind spot. You learn the place in order to last. Underfoot, in the flowering depths, there is only discord.

The world does not wait for you. Creatures follow hunger, threat, and a chance. Move a light, a crate, or a body, and the dungeon changes with you. Places, creatures, and objects answer by the same rules. There is no correct path. Your choices set the difficulty.

The origin

  1. 5+ years The tabletop book, more than 300 pages
  2. The launch The video game, a living dungeon

For more than five years, this world has been a tabletop role-playing book: an early-access PDF of more than 300 pages, written beside the setting itself.

The video game is what launches. The book is where the cultures, the moralities, and the rules were made playable first.
