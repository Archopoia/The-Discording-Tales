Weird fantasy TTRPGs - where The Discording Tales fits

A category page, not a hit piece. If you are shopping shelves for something stranger than default fantasy, this is the honest positioning.

The shelf

Weird fantasy and science-fantasy TTRPGs usually promise tone: body horror, surreal cities, cosmic dread, or gonzo tech. Many deliver atmosphere. Fewer deliver peoples as the engine - cultures you can argue with at the table, with relations and moralities that are not human cosplay.

What TDT optimizes for

  • No humans - you are always a guest in alien needs
  • Ethnographic density - ten peoples, lineages, and origin myths that are playable
  • System + world lock - lore is not a separate coffee-table book from the rules
  • Dual path - tabletop early-access book (300+ pages) and a first-person immersive sim Action RPG in development
  • Morrowind appetite, not a clone pitch - built for people still waiting on another Morrowind; supporting systems nods to Arx Fatalis, Gothic factions, Kenshi-scale world simulation
  • Bilingual hub - EN/FR site as the canonical discovery surface

What it is not

  • A drop-in D&D 5e setting with new monsters
  • Cozy pastoral fantasy
  • A Vvardenfell reskin - ethno-science-fantasy with no humans is the differentiator once the Morrowind hook lands
  • A finished AAA Action RPG you can buy today - the video path is in development; the tabletop book is already in early access

Who it is for

GMs and players who want cultural friction, golden-age strife, and a living dungeon of alien societies - and Action RPG players still waiting on another Morrowind. Investors and press who want the pitch and team should start at the landing.
